01

MIH Website

Overview

MIH (Moloele Investment Holdings) is a healthcare and technology investment company based in South Africa. This was a real client project that I worked on alongside a friend, where we collaborated to design and develop a professional website that reflected the company's brand, values and services.

Our goal was to create a modern and trustworthy online presence while moving away from the feel of a traditional corporate website. We developed an editorial, magazine-inspired visual direction and worked with MIH's existing blue and gold branding to create a consistent identity throughout the website.

Design & Development

I was heavily involved in the front-end development of the website, using HTML, CSS and JavaScript to turn our designs and the client's requirements into a functional digital experience. I focused on creating a clean layout, strong visual hierarchy and interactive elements that complemented the company's professional identity.

Responsive design was an important part of the development process. I adapted layouts, typography, navigation and individual sections for desktop, tablet and mobile devices rather than simply scaling down the desktop version of the website.

I also implemented interactive elements throughout the site, including scroll-based animations, hover effects, an interactive company timeline and contact functionality. Smaller details such as spacing, transitions and typography were refined throughout development to create a more polished user experience.

AI-Assisted Development

AI also formed part of my development workflow, particularly through tools such as Claude. I used AI to support problem-solving, debugging and exploring different approaches to implementing features using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Rather than simply using generated code, I reviewed, adapted and integrated suggestions into the existing codebase. This allowed me to use AI as a development tool to work more efficiently while remaining responsible for the final implementation, functionality and design decisions.

Collaboration & Client Work

Working alongside a friend gave me experience developing collaboratively. We shared ideas, divided responsibilities and reviewed our work throughout the project to maintain a consistent design and experience across the website.

We also communicated directly with the client throughout development, responding to feedback and making revisions as their requirements evolved. This gave me valuable experience balancing client expectations with design and development decisions.

Outcome

The final result was a fully responsive website developed and delivered for a real business. Taking the project from its initial concept through design, development, testing, client revisions and deployment strengthened my front-end development, problem-solving, collaboration and client communication skills.

02

Digital Doppelgänger

Overview

Digital Doppelgänger was my final-year university project, created to explore the relationship between people and their digital habits. The project was designed as an interactive web experience where users complete a questionnaire about their digital behaviour and receive a personalised result based on their responses.

Rather than creating a traditional survey website, I wanted to turn the user's answers into something more visual and interactive. The experience combines a multi-stage questionnaire, personalised results and a webcam-based visualisation that changes depending on the user's calculated digital behaviour profile.

Questionnaire & JavaScript Logic

I developed a five-stage questionnaire using HTML, CSS and JavaScript. User responses are stored using LocalStorage, allowing their answers to persist as they progress through the different stages of the questionnaire.

JavaScript processes the responses and calculates an overall score, which places the user into one of three categories: Healthy, Balanced or High. The results page then dynamically changes based on this outcome, presenting personalised information, different visual styling and a breakdown of the user's responses.

Interactive Visualisation

One of the main features of Digital Doppelgänger is an interactive webcam experience created using p5.js, ml5.js and PoseNet. Pose estimation is used to detect the user's position through their webcam and incorporate their movement into an interactive visual experience.

The appearance and behaviour of the visualisation changes depending on the result generated by the questionnaire. This connects the user's responses with a personalised visual output and allowed me to experiment with creative coding and browser-based machine learning within a web application.

Problem Solving

A key technical challenge during development was managing user data across multiple stages of the questionnaire and ensuring that the correct information reached the results and visualisation pages.

During development, I encountered an issue where questionnaire responses were not being stored correctly, preventing accurate results from being generated. I debugged the JavaScript responsible for storing and retrieving the responses, corrected the survey logic and tested each stage to ensure that the data persisted and produced the expected result.

Design & User Experience

The visual design was created around the idea of a digital identity, with the interface designed to make the questionnaire feel like part of an experience rather than a standard form. I considered layout, visual feedback, navigation and the transition between the questionnaire, results and visualisation to create a consistent user journey.

Outcome

Digital Doppelgänger became one of my most technically ambitious university projects, combining front-end development, JavaScript data handling, interactive visualisation and browser-based machine learning within a single experience.

The project strengthened my JavaScript and problem-solving skills while giving me the opportunity to experiment with technologies beyond a traditional website and develop an original idea into a functional interactive experience.
